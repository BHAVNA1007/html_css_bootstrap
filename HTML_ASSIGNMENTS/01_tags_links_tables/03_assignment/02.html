daily routine

  1. Wake Up & Get Ready

    Wake up in the morning, brush your teeth, take a bath, and get ready for the day.

  2. Study / Learning

    Spend some time learning new concepts, revising previous topics, and practicing skills.

  3. Household Chores

    Clean your room, wash clothes or dishes, and help with other daily household activities.

  4. Exercise / Walk

    Take a walk, do some stretching, or exercise for 20–30 minutes to stay active and fresh.

  5. Review & Plan for Tomorrow

    Review what you completed during the day and make a simple plan for the next day
